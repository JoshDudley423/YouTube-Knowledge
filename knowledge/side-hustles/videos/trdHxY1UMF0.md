# The Laziest AI Side Hustle Anyone Can Start Right Now
Channel: The Koerner Office | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trdHxY1UMF0 | Published: 2026-06-09

## Core idea: bilingual voicemail service for realtors (voice cloning)
- Clone a realtor's voice with an AI tool (Fish Audio), then generate pre-recorded voicemail templates in multiple languages (new listing alert, price drop, showing confirmation, offer accepted, counteroffer, inspection scheduled, closing reminder, purchase anniversary, referral request, holiday check-in).
- Pitch: ~40% of US home buyers speak a language other than English at home (over 50% in Dallas, NY, LA, Miami). Realtors can't currently leave voicemails in other languages in their own voice/tone — service fills that gap.
- Target buyer psychology: realtors win on speed-to-lead + "know, like, trust" — a voicemail in the recipient's native language, but still sounding like the realtor, builds trust/affinity.

## Process / steps
1. Sign up for Fish Audio ("Fish Audio Pro"). Clone a voice from as little as 10-60 seconds of audio (or reuse an existing IG/YouTube clip so the client doesn't have to record anything new).
2. Use an LLM (Claude/ChatGPT/etc.) to draft ~10 generic voicemail scripts (keep specifics like price/beds/baths as placeholders so they're reusable per listing), then have the LLM translate them into target languages — video used Spanish, Vietnamese, Mandarin, Portuguese (picked as most common non-English languages in the US); also suggests Italian, German, French, Hindi (growing Indian immigrant population).
3. Paste each translated script into Fish Audio's text-to-speech with the cloned voice, generate audio one at a time (batch generation combines files, so must do one-by-one to get separate downloadable files).
4. Organize output files in folders by language (e.g., Google Drive folder "voice cloning" > subfolder per language) and deliver to client.
5. Optional proactive close tactic: generate the demo set (e.g., 40 files = 10 messages x 4 languages) *before* pitching, then show the realtor the finished product to create a "wow" reaction and increase close rate.

## Numbers
- Fish Audio cost: $15 for 250,000 credits; generating the demo set used a fraction of a percent of credits (~3 cents or less per recording). Extremely cheap.
- Suggested pricing: $200-500/month per realtor or local business client (video opens with "$200/month," later suggests "$300-500/month is reasonable"); or one-time/loss-leader package to get in the door since recurring is a harder sell.
- Setup fee: $500-$1,000 per new client suggested as an add-on.
- Revenue examples given: 5 realtors x $300/mo = $1,500/mo ($18,000/yr); 10 realtors x $300/mo = $3,000/mo.
- Time cost: ~20-40 minutes of work per new client once workflow is set up.
- Audiobook narration variant: charge ~$500-a couple thousand dollars per book (cheaper than a studio, which can cost thousands); output quality meets Audible's technical specs.
- Local business voiceover agency variant: charge $200-500 per project (ads, hold messages, training videos, phone systems), same-day delivery.
- Cited stat: audio production industry worth $10B+ and growing; cost of production has dropped near zero while quality has risen.

## Other applications of the same voice-cloning workflow
- Audiobook narration for authors who can't afford/don't have time for studio narration.
- YouTube video dubbing into multiple languages (creator himself dubs into 14 languages to monetize ads in other countries) — service could be sold to other YouTubers, potentially niched by channel size (e.g., only channels with 100K-500K subs).
- General voiceover agency for local businesses (ads, phone hold messages, training videos) in the voice of the owner/receptionist.
- Also applicable to insurance agents, car dealerships, lawyers, medical offices, immigration attorneys (any business with multilingual clientele that leaves voicemails) — immigration attorneys specifically flagged as high-value targets (well-funded, entirely multilingual client base).

## Go-to-market plan suggested
- Day 1: clone own voice, generate 3 sample voicemails (English/Spanish/Chinese) as a demo.
- Day 2: reach out to realtors already in personal network (phone contacts, FB/IG friends), take them to lunch, clone their voice on the spot, demo it live for the "wow" effect. Testimonials from friends can be used as social proof for cold outreach even if they weren't paying customers.
- Alternative outreach: walk into local realtor/broker offices in person with a small gift (candy dish + business cards) — references an anecdote about someone building a six-figure junk removal business this way after prison.
- "Give away the secrets, sell the implementation": offer to teach realtors how to do it for free (most won't want to DIY) and leave pricing/QR code flyer; most will ask you to just do it for them.
- Days 3-7: follow up relentlessly with every contacted office/friend.
- Weeks 2-4: land first 3-5 clients, over-deliver/deliver faster than promised, collect testimonials and 5-star Google Business reviews.
- Month 2+: expand into adjacent verticals (audiobooks, insurance agents, immigration attorneys) using the same service with a different script.

## Caveats/risks
- AI-cloned voice speaking a foreign language doesn't sound like a true native speaker (host demoed this with Spanish/Mandarin — accent is noticeably imperfect, verb conjugation not perfect) but is still framed as good enough to build rapport/affinity.
- Recurring monthly revenue is harder to sell than one-time; author suggests treating the first project as a loss-leader/foot-in-the-door rather than assuming recurring revenue is guaranteed.
- Requires securing rights/permission to use someone's voice when cloning it (tool has a rights-grant step).
