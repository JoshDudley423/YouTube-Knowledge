# Google Veo 3 Just Changed the Game: Here's Your Money-Making Tutorial
Channel: The Koerner Office | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jzcuxz7IeR0 | Published: 2025-05-25

How to monetize Google's Veo 3 AI video generation tool (Gemini's text-to-video model, publicly launched at time of filming).

- **Tool cost**: $250/month, 50% off for first 3 months (~$133 first month including tax). Differentiator vs. Sora/Kling/Pika/Runway per the host: real camera movement, shallow depth of field, baked-in sound design/voices/ambient audio -- "the first AI filmmaker," not just a visual generator.
- **Who will pay for AI-generated video services** (target customers):
  1. **Brands/ecommerce** (Shopify stores, fitness, skincare, supplements, coaching) needing constant ad/social content -- sell AI-made "UGC" style content. Pricing: $100-$300 per video, or $500-$1,000/month for 4-8 videos.
  2. **Video production agencies/marketing agencies** -- "sneaky play": offer to generate assets for them without a crew, undercutting their cost while they keep client relationships. One agency client could be worth $1,000-$4,000/month recurring without touching the end client.
  3. **Real estate agents** -- cinematic drone flyovers, interior B-roll, neighborhood lifestyle shots for $200 instead of the $2,000+ they'd normally pay.
  4. **Stock footage** -- generate 5-10 second hyper-specific niche clips (e.g. "chef cutting herbs in rustic kitchen") and upload to Pond5, Adobe Stock, Art List; low-effort, no outreach needed.
  5. **Local service businesses** (roofers, landscapers, med spas, dog groomers, tree trimmers) -- cinematic before/after reels with their logo, sold as a monthly content package; can be sent unsolicited/on spec before they even know you.
- **Cold-outreach script for agencies**: casual DM/email framing it as "not sure if your team is experimenting with AI video tools yet... happy to show examples... could save your clients thousands." Advises not over-explaining the tech (avoid making the recipient feel dumb) and to DM agency owners personally on Instagram (less crowded than cold email). Claims 10-30% response rate sending to 10-20 agency owners.
- **Building a demo portfolio in a weekend**: generate 3-5 polished 5-10 second demo clips in a niche (fitness/food/lifestyle suggested) with background music, no branding, hosted on Google Drive/Vimeo. Tip: browse sora.com's "Explore" page (filter by Top/Month/Video) to see viral prompts and the exact prompt text used -- feed those prompts into ChatGPT to reverse-engineer "first principles" of what makes them effective.
- **Recurring revenue model / productized offer**: flat monthly pricing beats per-video pricing psychologically (e.g. "$500/month for 4 custom videos" outperforms "$50/video" because people want predictable bills). Suggested customer segments: influencers/creators (become their "quiet video team"), small businesses (gyms, med spas, coffee shops, chiropractors) at $300-$800/month, or white-labeled to agencies who resell at 2x. Recommends niching to one vertical (e.g. only chiropractors) to reuse the same content/prompts across clients, cutting production time.
- **Path with zero clients/audience** (his stated "if I had to start over" plan): buy Veo access -> generate 3 demo videos in fitness/food/fashion -> cold DM 30 people (15 agencies, 15 brands) with "I made this with AI, mind if I send examples?" -> offer a $100 test video with 48-hour turnaround to first responders -> reinvest that money into 30 more targeted DMs -> after 2-3 paid videos, package into a $500/month, 4-videos/month offer.
- **Non-client / low-touch monetization paths**:
  - **Prompt packs**: bundle 10 ready-to-use Veo prompts per niche (weddings, fitness, travel, luxury) with example videos, sell on Gumroad/Notion/Etsy for ~$29.
  - **Stock footage** (see above) via Pond5/Art List/Adobe Stock.
  - **Courses/tutorials**: micro-course teaching Veo prompting, e.g. $49, or bundled with a prompt pack for $79.
  - **Paid newsletter**: his own newsletter grew to 154,000 subscribers over ~1.5 years; suggests growing via viral content or paid Facebook/Instagram ads, then adding a paid tier and eventually a private Discord/Slack community ($10-$20/month) for prompt access/feedback, with upsells into consulting or equity stakes.
- **Upsell path from single videos to bigger retainers**: once a client pays for one Veo video, ask "want me to handle the rest of your content too?" -- expand into a package (4 Veo videos/month repurposed into Reels/TikTok/Shorts/Facebook, branded thumbnails, captions, optimized posting, optionally +1 landing page/email) priced around $1,000/month, pitched against the cost of a social media manager (2-4x more expensive). Work can be outsourced (Canva templates, CapCut, VAs, ChatGPT, Gumloop, Lindy automation tools) so the seller acts as "creative director" rather than doing everything personally. Scaling claim: 1 client pays back the tool cost 4x; 5 clients = quit-your-job money; 10 clients = six figures/year.
- **Bonus idea -- voice licensing (passive income)**: record voice samples (in varied tones -- excited, nervous, agitated) at 11labs.io (ElevenLabs) and list on their voice marketplace; buyers include audiobook producers, YouTube channels, customer support bots. Unusual/accented voices reportedly do better ("anything normal sounds AI-generated"). Reported earnings: commonly hundreds per month, some over $1,000/month for about an hour of upfront recording work; can niche into specific personas (Gen Z narrator, meditation voice, luxury brand tone).

Caveats: host explicitly frames this as a time-sensitive, low-competition window ("in six months to 12 months, people won't be shocked and you'll have more competition") -- urges immediate action over perfecting the idea. Cites one Reddit anecdote (a commercial producer who normally charges $500,000 for pharma ads made a comparable video for $500 in credits/half a day and is now quoting $10-15K for AI-made commercials) as unverified but illustrative.
