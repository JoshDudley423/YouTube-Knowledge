# The Laziest One-Person Business You Can Start
Channel: The Koerner Office | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6u7tkj7UJvs | Published: 2026-07-14

Solo episode by Chris Koerner demoing Higgsfield's "Supercomputer" tool (sponsored) — this is a tools/tactics video for running an AI-driven content and business-building operation, not a guest interview.

## The idea(s)
- Use an autonomous AI creative tool (Higgsfield Supercomputer, built on an agent framework called "Hermes") to research trending content, generate a complete brand/marketing package, and produce video content for a business with minimal manual input — pitched as a way to run a one-person AI implementation/consulting agency (or similar low-overhead service) largely hands-off.
- Core business idea demoed live: an "AI agency" that does paid AI audits as a lead-in, then charges $1,000-$5,000/month to help small-to-medium businesses integrate AI tools (Chris names the fictitious brand "Firepower" on the spot).

## What the tool does (process demoed)
1. Connect Higgsfield to a personal Claude account via a custom MCP connector (Settings > Connectors > Add custom connector, enter Higgsfield's MCP URL) so Supercomputer can use your Claude context/history.
2. Use the "trend picker" skill: prompt it (e.g., "show me viral trends and content in the business ideas niche") and it scans TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram (took ~3 minutes) to return: top viral content in the niche over the last 7 days, long-form and short-form trend breakdowns by topic/hashtag, winning content formats/hooks, key creators to watch, and ad-spend insights. Chris notes this works for any niche (gardening, sewing, family lifestyle, shopping, travel, etc.), not just his own.
3. Start a business inside Supercomputer: describe the business in a sentence or two (service, pricing model, name) and let it run autonomously rather than asking clarifying questions — it defines brand identity (name, positioning, messaging, colors, typography), generates marketing images/flyers (e.g., a "free AI audit" flyer), a website mockup and full website as a downloadable zip file, a "brand bible," a content marketing campaign playbook PDF, and even bonus assets not explicitly requested (e.g., a "5 AI tools every CEO should know" pamphlet). Chris compares this output to what a $150K-$350K/year CMO would produce, for a monthly software cost instead.
4. Generate short-form video ads: prompt it to make faceless TikTok-style videos (20-35 seconds) promoting the business; it produces finished video ads from a one-line prompt.
5. "Clone" a specific viral video: paste the URL of an existing viral TikTok/video in your niche and ask Supercomputer to generate an original video "inspired by" it in your own style/brand — it analyzes the source video's visual style, text overlays, and pacing, then produces a new video following that same "recipe." Demoed on a 1.3M-view TikTok about ChatGPT prompt hacks.
6. Paid "boost credits" are available to speed up processing if you don't want to wait for the free-tier generation queue.
7. (Mentioned but not demoed in this video) Supercomputer can connect directly to your social platforms and auto-distribute generated content with one click.

## Business tactics suggested using this tool (Chris's own suggestions, not just demoed)
- **Real estate ad machine**: take a real estate agent's listing, feed it into Supercomputer to generate a full marketing package (graphics, copy, social posts) for that single listing, give it to the agent for free as a foot-in-the-door — likely converts into paid work on their future listings.
- **"Product hunt arbitrage"**: find a small content creator (as few as 5,000 followers) who isn't yet monetizing / doesn't have a product, build them a free content plan and generated videos, and DM it to them unsolicited to demonstrate value before pitching paid work.
- **AI virtual influencer**: create a fictional AI "influencer" persona (character visuals + backstory) and have Supercomputer generate a full month's content calendar for it; if it's not gaining traction after a month, pivot to a different persona/angle.
- General framing: an AI-implementation/consulting business charging ~$3,000/month per client only needs 10 clients to reach $30,000/month with no employees — the constraint isn't the software/product (easier than ever to build), it's distribution (sales/marketing), which is the gap this tool is pitched as closing.

## Caveats / risks
- This is a sponsored demo of a paid tool; no independent verification of business outcomes (e.g., no real client results, revenue, or retention data reported for a business built this way).
- The "clone a viral video" tactic explicitly involves copying another creator's established format/style ("recipe") rather than fully original content — a reuse/derivative-content strategy, not novel IP.
