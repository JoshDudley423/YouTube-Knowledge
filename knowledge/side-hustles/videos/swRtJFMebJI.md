# Alex Hormozi being a business genius for 10 minutes straight
Channel: The Koerner Office | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=swRtJFMebJI | Published: 2024-07-24

Compilation of Alex Hormozi clips curated by Chris Koerner. This is almost entirely mindset/motivational/leadership philosophy, not a business idea or a how-to. No specific numbers, tools, platforms, or step-by-step process for starting a side hustle are given. The handful of substantive business-strategy points made:

- **Pricing/positioning strategy**: cites Tesla's launch strategy as deliberately starting at the premium end of the market (the original Roadster at $250,000) rather than trying to serve the mass market first — argued as generally easier ("a lot of good for a small amount of people" vs. "a little good for a large amount of people").
- **Low-price/high-volume model requires infrastructure built for scale from day one**: if you compete on low price, you only make money via very high volume, which means you must build the operational infrastructure for huge demand up front (cites Walmart and Amazon as businesses built for volume from the start that win on operating efficiency, not margin per unit).
- **Base rate reality check**: "90% of businesses never hit a million dollars a year in sales" — cited as a reason not to take business advice/judgment from people who haven't actually built anything themselves.
- **Organizational structure claim**: at scale, "all businesses at the top" reduce to the same functional roles regardless of what's being sold (widgets, services, or software) — marketing, sales, product, finance, legal, tech — implying that as a side hustle grows into a real company, you should expect to eventually need people in each of these functions.
- **Hiring heuristic**: "always hire ahead of where you're at" (hire for the scale you're growing into, not just current-state needs).
- **Iteration/testing point**: reframes learning as "same condition, new behavior" — if you keep trying the same approach and getting the same (bad) result, you have not actually learned/adjusted, borrowing a video-game boss-fight analogy.

## Caveats
- This video is a curated highlight reel of general entrepreneurial mindset advice, not a specific side-hustle or business-idea pitch — there is no product, market, cost structure, or revenue example described. Treat it as background philosophy rather than an actionable playbook.
