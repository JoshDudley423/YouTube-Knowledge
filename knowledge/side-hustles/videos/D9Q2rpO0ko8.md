# The Best Low Budget Online Business To Start Right Now
Channel: The Koerner Office | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9Q2rpO0ko8 | Published: 2025-12-11

Tutorial/pitch for a specific low-cost service business: **"database reactivation" agency** — selling small businesses a service that re-engages their old customer list via email/SMS to generate fast revenue, built on the GoHighLevel (HighLevel) CRM platform.

## The business idea
- Service: take a small business's existing customer list (from QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, Square, or any point-of-sale/payment tool that captures emails) and run an automated reactivation email/SMS campaign to drive incremental revenue.
- Framed as an ideal "foot in the door" no-brainer offer to land a first client, which can then be upsold into ongoing/higher-value services.
- Tool: GoHighLevel (HighLevel) — has a pre-built "list reactivation" workflow template (drag-and-drop, no custom build needed). Video shows a 30-day free trial link (host's affiliate link vs. the standard 14-day trial).

## Concrete numbers / worked example (tree trimming business)
- Example list: 4,000 previous customers.
- Assumed conversion funnel: 2% of list responds requesting a quote (80 people) → ~50% close rate (40 jobs) → $1,000 average job size = $40,000 in generated revenue.
- Agency pricing model: charge 10% of revenue generated ($4,000 in this example) for essentially the cost of sending one templated email campcampaign.
- Alternative/hybrid pricing to solve the "no visibility into whether client actually closed the job" problem: charge a flat fee (e.g., $1,000) OR 10% of revenue, whichever is less — guarantees minimum payment regardless of client's reporting honesty.
- In businesses where the outcome is externally verifiable (e.g., roofing — you can literally drive by and see if a new roof was installed), percentage-of-revenue pricing is more trustworthy; cited example: an email to 1,000 past roofing customers might generate only 1-3 jobs but that could be $20,000-$50,000 in revenue.
- Suggested opening approach: consider doing the first campaign completely free to prove value and open the door to upsell additional services.

## Process / step-by-step (tool-specific)
1. In GoHighLevel: Automation → Create Workflow → Select from Template → search "reactivation" → choose the "list reactivation" template.
2. Import the client's customer list via Contacts → Import Contacts (map columns: name, email, phone, address, etc.; tag the import batch).
3. Set the automation trigger off that tag (or alternatively off pipeline-stage changes, or a time delay after a payment event, e.g., "365 days after Stripe payment processed").
4. Build the email/SMS content with merge fields (e.g., first name) and a specific offer (free quote, % off, buy-2-get-1-free, etc.).
5. Automation logic: waits ~1 hour for a reply, uses sentiment detection to branch on positive vs. non-positive replies, and can auto-respond with a booking link (Calendly, HighLevel's smart calendar, Tidycal) without manual intervention.
6. Built-in analytics dashboard (Marketing → Emails) shows delivered/bounced/unsubscribed/spam/open/click/reply rates — useable as "proof of work" screenshots to send the client.
7. Built-in A/B testing: use the workflow's "random split" node (e.g., 50/50) to test two different offers on portions of the same list simultaneously; per-variant stats available within about an hour of sending, letting the agency identify which offer performs best for a given industry (e.g., a tree-trimming offer that wins can be reused as-is across other tree-trimming clients).

## Upsell / adjacent service ideas named
Once the client is engaged, additional services to sell: after-hours AI voice agents, missed-call text-back automation, review request automation, review response automation, AI appointment setting, lead nurturing sequences, custom chatbots.

## Caveats
- Business owner clients typically won't know what their customer list even is or where to find it — must proactively suggest common sources (QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, Square, POS systems).
- Percentage-of-revenue pricing has a real trust/verification problem unless the outcome is independently observable; plan pricing structure accordingly.
- Video functions partly as an affiliate promotion for GoHighLevel and the "Playmakers AI" consulting community (charging $500-$5k/month) — treat platform-specific claims accordingly.
