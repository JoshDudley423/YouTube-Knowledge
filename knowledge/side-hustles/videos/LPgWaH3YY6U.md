- Business idea: use the AI website builder **Wegic** (wegic.ai) to build full websites from a single prompt, either for your own business or as a paid **web design/agency service** for other business owners — pitched as a way to save clients (or yourself) $3,000-$5,000 vs. hiring a traditional web designer.
- Demonstrated process (pickleball club site, "Secret Pickleball Club"):
  1. Give Wegic one detailed prompt describing the business, required features (court booking system, leaderboard, merch/upsell section), and target users.
  2. Wegic asks clarifying questions (primary goal, target audience segments, whether to require membership to buy merch, payment processing needs) before generating.
  3. Full site (including hover/animation effects, gradient styling, booking + leaderboard structure) generated in about **2.5 minutes** from the initial prompt.
  4. Iterate via natural-language prompts to add specific interaction design (e.g., custom hover/rotation/scale animations on product images) — Wegic offers multiple stylistic variants to choose from per request.
  5. Text/copy changes (e.g., changing the headline) can be bundled into the same prompt as design changes without breaking the tool.
  6. Built-in responsive/mobile optimization — confirmed the layout auto-adapts for mobile.
  7. Click "Publish" to go live; a real MVP was published in roughly 1 minute of total build time.
- Second demo (restaurant, "Secret Pizza," Dallas, TX): one prompt specifying business type/location, payment integration (so the owner isn't dependent on DoorDash), homepage menu, hero image (a specific "cheesy pull" pepperoni slice photo direction), founder photo section, and fake 5-star reviews to swap later — full site generated in **1 minute** (timed on camera: 8:04 to 8:05).
  - AI generated realistic food photography and founder photos from the text prompt alone (no stock/uploaded images).
  - Follow-up prompts fixed missing payment button wiring ("change the four order now buttons to payment buttons keeping the original color") and edited pricing directly in the UI.
  - Built-in AI chat/contact form on the site correctly handled an out-of-script customer request (asked for "gluten-free only" pizza) without being explicitly programmed for it, and routed the lead into an inbox.
  - Wegic flagged that the reviews were fake (since he explicitly asked for fake ones) — a content-honesty guardrail to be aware of.
- Tools/platforms named: Wegic (wegic.ai) — free to start, first website is free; Wegic 2.0 mentioned as a recent upgrade. Other UI features noted: "connect and refresh content" (auto-pull from an external blog/news/Twitter feed), a "batch adjust" tool for bulk image resizing/filtering, drag-to-reorder sections, and a credit-based full-section regeneration feature.
- Advice for building a web-design side hustle/agency on top of this:
  - Pick an industry you personally understand or have worked in — gives you a real edge in knowing what to build and sell.
  - Business owners often don't know their own pain points outright; get to specifics by asking open "who/what/when/where/why" follow-up questions about their current process rather than "what are your problems?"
  - Because AI makes iteration nearly free (4 more prompts = 4 new website variants, vs. hours of manual rework), you can offer clients multiple design directions cheaply.
  - Pricing advice: charge for the value delivered, not time spent — don't undercut traditional agencies (e.g., $3,000) just because your delivery is faster/cheaper for you; speed-to-delivery itself can justify equal or higher pricing.
